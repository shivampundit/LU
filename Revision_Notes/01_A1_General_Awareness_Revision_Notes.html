📌 1.1 Indus Valley Civilization (IVC) Major Sites & Archaeological Finds
Key Formulas & Rules
  • 🔹 Chronology: Mature Harappan Phase = 2600 BCE – 1900 BCE (Radiocarbon C-14 dating)
Simple Exam Points
  • Urban bronze-age civilization featuring grid-pattern town planning, burnt-brick underground drainage, Great Bath & Granary at Mohenjo-daro (Larkana, Indus), tidal dockyard & rice husks at Lothal (Bhogava river, Gujarat), 3-part citadel & giant water reservoirs with 10-sign signboard at Dholavira (Luni river, Rann of Kutch — UNESCO 40th site), ploughed field & fire altars at Kalibangan (Ghaggar, Rajasthan), and terracotta plough at Banawali.
✅ Direct Exam Example: Matched Pair Verification: Lothal -> Artificial Brick Dockyard (Gujarat); Kalibangan -> Furrowed/Ploughed Field & Black Bangles (Rajasthan); Dholavira -> Rock-cut Water Management System & 3-tier town division.
💡 Exam Shortcut / Trap: Exam Trap: Iron (Loha) aur Horse-drawn spoked chariots IVC me unknown the; Harappan log Bronze (Copper + Tin) use karte the aur bullock carts/boats se Mesopotamia (Meluha) ke sath trade karte the.

📌 1.5 Mughal Administration, Mansabdari & Land Revenue Systems
Key Formulas & Rules
  • 🔹 Todar Mal's Bandobast / Dahsala System (1580 CE): 10-year average produce & prices; 1/3rd state share
Simple Exam Points
  • Akbar introduced the Mansabdari system with dual ranks: 'Zat' (personal status and salary scale of noble) and 'Sawar' (actual number of cavalrymen maintained).
  • Land was classified into 4 categories under Dahsala: Polaj (cultivated every year), Parauti (left fallow 1–2 years), Chachar (fallow 3–4 years), and Banjar (uncultivated 5+ years).
  • Sher Shah Suri built Grand Trunk Road (Sadak-e-Azam), issued Silver Rupiya (178 grains), and introduced Patta & Qabuliyat.
✅ Direct Exam Example: Battle Milestones: 1st Panipat (1526, Babur vs Ibrahim Lodi) -> Khanwa (1527, Babur vs Rana Sanga) -> 2nd Panipat (1556, Akbar/Bairam Khan vs Hemu) -> Haldighati (1576).
💡 Exam Shortcut / Trap: Exam Trap: 'Zat' personal rank aur salary fix karta tha, jabki 'Sawar' ghudsawar (cavalry) contingent ki sankhya batata tha. Du-Aspa Sih-Aspa Jahangir ne introduce kiya tha!
